Give brief details about your project and tech stacks on which you worked?
Before moving code into the production environment, what checks, guidelines, environments, and coverage are there? 
What tools are used for these production-readiness checks?
Explain one critical challenge you faced and what strategies you built to resolved it?
What are the different design patterns used in microservices? 
How do you handle authentication and authorization in microservices? 
Explain in detail how a token is generated, how it is validated, and what is the complete process involved. 
How does one microservice connect with another microservice? How do microservices communicate with each other?
If your application performance is slow, how would you optimize it and make sure that it works as expected in production? 
How is authentication and authorization maintained in Spring Boot? 
What are the different ways to create a thread in Java, and which approach is feasible/preferred? 
How do threads work with conditions / thread condition handling? 
What is a race condition? How can we avoid a race condition? 
What is the difference between checked and unchecked exceptions? 
What is the difference between String Buffer and StringBuilder?
