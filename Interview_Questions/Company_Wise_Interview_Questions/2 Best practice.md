

Not sure about Deloitte, but this is the interview pattern followed by most other companies using HYRGPT AI.

In HYRGPT AI, the interview starts with a self-introduction, so make sure to mention your technology stack clearly.

* After the introduction, the AI asks technical questions based on the technology stack you mentioned. Each question is displayed on the screen in both voice and text format.
* Every question may have one or more follow-up questions based on your response.
* After recording your answer, you'll have the option to review and edit it in text format before submitting.
* Each question has a fixed time limit, so answer within the allotted time.

Note:

* Maintain eye contact with the camera throughout the interview.
* Do not read the question aloud. Answer naturally, as if you're speaking to a real interviewer.
