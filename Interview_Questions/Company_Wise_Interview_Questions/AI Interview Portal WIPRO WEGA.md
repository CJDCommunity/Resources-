Wipro AI Screening Round – Interview Experience & Guidelines

1. Introduction (Maximum 1 Minute)

The AI interviewer will first ask you to introduce yourself. Keep your introduction concise, structured, and relevant to the position you have applied for.

Tip: Focus on your total experience, key technical skills, domain expertise, and current project responsibilities. Do not exceed one minute.

2. Scenario-Based Technical Questions

The AI interviewer will ask questions related to the job position and the technical keywords mentioned in your resume.

The questions are scenario-based rather than basic theoretical questions. The AI will select specific statements from your resume and ask you to explain how you implemented them in real-world projects.

Example from a resume:

“Engineered a high-performance Spring Batch framework for processing 10M+ banking records daily with automatic restartability, checkpointing, and fault tolerance.”

Possible AI follow-up question:

“How did you design and implement the Spring Batch framework to process 10 million banking records daily? Explain how you handled checkpointing, restartability, fault tolerance, and performance optimization.”

Important: Be prepared to explain every technical achievement, project responsibility, and implementation detail mentioned in your resume. The interviewer may ask follow-up questions to validate your practical experience and technical understanding.

3. Voice-Based Response Only

- The AI interviewer will ask questions through voice, and the corresponding question will also appear as text on the screen.
- Candidates are required to answer using voice input only.
- You will not be allowed to edit your response as text after speaking.
- Listen carefully to the complete question before answering.
- Structure your response clearly and explain your actual implementation approach.

4. Maintain Eye Contact with the Camera

- Look directly at the camera while answering.
- Avoid repeatedly looking away from the screen.
- Maintain a confident posture and professional body language.
- Speak clearly, at a moderate pace, and with confidence.

5. Preparation Strategy

Before starting the assessment, review your resume thoroughly, especially the technical achievements and project descriptions.

For each major statement in your resume, prepare to explain:

1. Problem statement: What business or technical problem were you solving?
2. Implementation: How did you implement the solution?
3. Technical decisions: Why did you choose that approach or technology?
4. Challenges: What issues did you face, and how did you resolve them?
5. Results: What measurable improvements or outcomes did your solution achieve?
