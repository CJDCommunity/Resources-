🗄️ SQL  
  
Given:  
  
* employee(id, name, dept_id, salary, status)  
* department(id, dept_name)  
  
Write a query to find the department-wise employee count and total salary for ACTIVE employees, including department name, sorted by total salary in descending order.  
  
💻 Coding Questions  
  
1. Find Duplicate Numbers  
  
Given the array:  
  
[1, 2, 3, 2, 4, 5, 1, 6]  
  
Return the numbers that appear more than once. Also explain an optimized approach and its time complexity.

You have an empty 5-litre jug and an empty 3-litre jug, with unlimited water available.  
  
How would you measure exactly 4 litres?
