HiperBrain AI Screening Round – Interview Experience & Guidelines

1. Assessment Structure

The recruiter will divide the assessment into multiple sections, depending on the requirements of the company.

Section A: MCQs and SBQs
- This section consists of Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs) and Scenario-Based Questions (SBQs).
- Questions will be based on the technical skills and competencies required for the position.

Section B: Hard-Level Coding Round
- Candidates will be required to solve 2–3 coding questions.
- The coding questions will be of HARD difficulty level, as decided by the hiring company.
- Expect challenging problems involving data structures, algorithms, problem-solving, and optimization.
- Focus on selecting an efficient approach, handling edge cases, and optimizing time and space complexity.
- Ensure your solution handles large inputs and hidden test cases within the given time constraints.

Section C: AI Interview
- The AI bot will ask questions verbally, and the questions will also be displayed on the screen.
- A timer will start for each response, so candidates must answer within the allotted time.
- Once a response has been recorded, you may not be able to exit or modify it. Be prepared before starting your answer.
- Listen carefully to each question and provide a clear, structured response within the time limit.

2. Camera, Lighting, and Noise Requirements

- Ensure the room is well-lit so your face is clearly visible to the camera.
- Sit in a quiet environment without background noise.
- Even fan noise may be detected by the system and potentially flagged as suspicious activity or malpractice.
- Position your camera properly and ensure your face remains visible throughout the assessment.
- Avoid unnecessary movements and distractions during the interview.

3. Resume-Based and Cloud-Related Questions

The AI interviewer may ask scenario-based questions based on the technical skills, project descriptions, and achievements mentioned in your resume.

Questions may also cover cloud technologies and concepts relevant to the job description.

For example, if your resume mentions that you developed Spring Boot microservices deployed on AWS or Azure, you may be asked:

- How did you deploy your microservices to the cloud?
- How did you manage application configuration and secrets?
- How did you monitor application performance and troubleshoot production issues?
- How did you handle scalability, fault tolerance, and service availability?
- How did you secure communication between services and cloud resources?

Important: Prepare to explain your actual implementation, architecture, technical decisions, challenges, and troubleshooting approach rather than memorizing theoretical definitions.

4. Preparation Strategy

Before starting the assessment:

1. Review the job description and your resume thoroughly.
2. Practice MCQs and scenario-based technical questions relevant to your role.
3. Practice HARD-level coding problems involving advanced data structures, algorithms, and optimization.
4. Prepare detailed explanations for the projects and technical achievements listed on your resume.
5. Revise cloud concepts and services relevant to your experience and the job requirements.
6. Test your camera, microphone, lighting, and internet connection.
7. Keep your verbal answers concise and structured to fit within the allotted time.

Final Recommendation

Treat the HiperBrain assessment as a combination of technical screening, hard-level coding evaluation, and a time-bound AI interview. Focus on advanced problem-solving, resume-based scenarios, cloud knowledge, and clear verbal communication. Ensure your environment is quiet and well-lit before beginning the assessment.
