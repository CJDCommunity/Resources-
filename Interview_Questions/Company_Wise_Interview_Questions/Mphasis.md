Interview Mphasis

Okay, so I have around 5+ years of experience. I gave the Mphasis interview around 3 months ago. There were 3 rounds 2 technical rounds and 1 client round, which was also technical.

First round - One coding question related to Array (LeetCode Easy), and then some questions on Microservices like how they communicate, difference between sync and async communication, how circuit breaker works, etc. They asked about Kafka components and the complete flow. They also asked about design patterns,which design pattern I used in my project and why. Some questions on Garbage Collector, memory management, and how indexing works in database (which I was not able to answer).

Second round - They asked me to explain my last project's architecture and question around it. Then questions like what is Kafka retention policy, internal working of ConcurrentHashMap, how do you handle duplicate messages in Kafka, etc. They also asked some AWS questions (again, I was not able to answer 😅) and asked me to write one SQL query related to INNER JOIN.

Client round - Again, explain your last project. They asked about any major feature I worked on, what type of bugs generally come in the project, and how I handle/debug them. Then  questions on AWS. Finally, I was able to answer those. And again some question on Microservices, Kafka.
Overall, my experience was nice, but the whole interview process took around 3 weeks.
