
🚀 Crunchyroll SDE-2 Interview Experience | ~3.5 YOE  
  
Preparing for SDE-2 or Backend Software Engineer interviews at product-based companies? Here’s an interview experience covering DSA, project deep dives, system design, and cross-functional discussions.  
  
📌 Round 1 — DSA (Karat)  
  
The round focused on problem-solving, algorithmic thinking, and explaining the solution.  
  
🔹 Two DSA problems  
🔹 Graphs and Dynamic Programming  
🔹 Time and space complexity  
🔹 Explaining the approach and optimizing the solution  
  
📌 Round 2 — Project Deep Dive  
  
This round focused on previous project experience and technical ownership.  
  
🔹 Explain your previous project’s architecture.  
🔹 What were your key responsibilities and contributions?  
🔹 Why did you choose a particular design or technology?  
🔹 What challenges did you face, and how did you solve them?  
🔹 Explain important implementation decisions and their trade-offs.  
  
📌 Round 3 — System Design  
  
Design a video streaming platform similar to Crunchyroll.  
  
Important areas to cover:  
  
🔹 Overall architecture, components, and data flow  
🔹 API design for video uploads, processing, and playback  
🔹 Database schema and technology selection  
🔹 Video storage and metadata management  
🔹 Video processing and transcoding pipelines  
🔹 CDN and content delivery  
🔹 Caching strategies  
🔹 Handling concurrent users and high traffic  
🔹 Scalability, reliability, and system design trade-offs  
  
📌 Round 4 — Cross-Functional Discussion  
  
This round focused on product thinking and collaboration.  
  
🔹 How do you approach product requirements?  
🔹 How do you handle cross-team dependencies?  
🔹 How do you communicate with different stakeholders?  
🔹 How do you balance technical decisions with business needs?  
  
📌 Round 5 — Hiring Manager  
  
🔹 Discussion about overall experience and previous projects  
🔹 Behavioural questions  
🔹 Career decisions and professional growth  
  
🎉 Final Verdict: Selected!  
  
💡 Key Takeaways for SDE-2 Aspirants  
  
✅ Practise DSA, especially graphs and dynamic programming.  
✅ Know your project architecture and technical contributions in depth.  
✅ Practise designing scalable systems such as video streaming platforms.  
✅ Explain trade-offs instead of simply listing technologies.  
✅ Develop strong product thinking, communication, and collaboration skills.
