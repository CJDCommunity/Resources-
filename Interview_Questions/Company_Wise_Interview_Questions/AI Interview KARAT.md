
KARAT – CITI BANK Interview Preparation & Guidelines
Interview Type: Live Technical Interview & Coding Assessment
Platform: KARAT
Expected Duration: Approximately 60 Minutes

The Citi Bank KARAT interview evaluates Core Java knowledge, practical coding skills, debugging abilities, problem-solving approach, and communication skills.
1. Self Introduction (3–5 Minutes)
The interviewer will ask you to introduce yourself and explain your professional experience.

Preparation Strategy:
- Keep your introduction concise.
- Mention your total years of experience.
- Highlight your primary technical skills.
- Briefly explain your project and domain expertise.
- Mention your role and key responsibilities.
2. Technical Discussion (10–15 Minutes)
The interviewer may ask technical questions based on your resume, project experience, and practical implementation.

Preparation Strategy:
- Understand every technical statement in your resume.
- Explain your actual project implementation.
- Prepare real-world technical challenges and solutions.
- Focus on practical knowledge rather than memorized definitions.
3. Live Coding & Debugging (30–35 Minutes)
Assessment Type: Interviewer-Led Live Coding

The interviewer will share a coding problem or existing Java codebase on the KARAT platform.

You will be expected to understand the problem, explain your approach, implement the solution, and validate your code.

Expected Process:
1. Understand the problem statement.
2. Clarify requirements and edge cases.
3. Explain your approach before coding.
4. Implement the solution.
5. Debug existing code if required.
6. Execute test cases and validate results.
7. Explain time and space complexity.
Important Live Coding Guidelines
- Keep explaining your implementation while coding.
- Do not remain silent during the assessment.
- Use clean and readable Java code.
- Compile and test your code frequently.
- Handle null values, duplicates, and edge cases.
- Explain your logic and optimization decisions.
Important: The interviewer evaluates both your coding skills and your ability to communicate your thought process.
4. Testing & Optimization (5–10 Minutes)
- Execute sample test cases.
- Validate edge cases.
- Identify and fix logical errors.
- Discuss performance improvements.
- Explain time and space complexity.
