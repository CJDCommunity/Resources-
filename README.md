## 👋 Welcome to CJD Community

We're a growing community of **Java developers** helping each other crack interviews, level up on the job, and grow their careers. Everything here is **free, open, and built by the community, for the community**.

> 💡 *"The best way to learn is to teach. The best way to grow is together."*

## 🤝 How to contribute

1. 🍴 **Fork** the [Resources](https://github.com/CJDCommunity/Resources-) repo
2. 📂 Add your material to the **right folder** (no duplicates, please)
3. 🏷️ Give **proper credit** to original authors
4. 🔁 Open a **pull request** and we'll review it

Shared an interview recently? **Your experience can help someone land their dream job.** 💼
